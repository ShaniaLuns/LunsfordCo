THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

A little about me

Hello, I'm
Shania Lunsford.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer interested in the ways visual ideas bring people, places, and stories together.

My practice moves between graphic design, brand identity, illustration, and digital experiences. I’m drawn to thoughtful concepts, expressive color, and the details that make a design feel considered and human.

Whether I’m building a visual identity or creating materials for a community project, my goal is to make work that feels purposeful, memorable, and true to the people it serves.
